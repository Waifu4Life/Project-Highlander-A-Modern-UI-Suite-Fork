# Project Highlander 3.61.0 — Milestone build

A Modern UI Suite fork for Gen 1 (Red/Blue/Yellow) and Gen 2 (Gold/Silver/Crystal).
This milestone rounds up everything since the 3.44.0 milestone.

## Start Menu
- New FULL SCREEN style (default): a whole 160x144 page in the cart's own font
  and four shades, with a clock bar, 2 x 5 icon-and-name tiles and a button-hint
  footer. QUIT always sits bottom-right. With the DEVICE clock the bar shows the
  full date and time ("Sun 2026-10-18 23:50"). Custom Borders frame it like the
  Title, Trainer ID, Battle, Options and Mods screens. PHONE keeps the old panel.
- New 16px icon set for the full-screen menu; 24x24 icons for the phone panel.

## Party and Bag
- Party header: "6/6 Pokémon" on the left and both types spelled out on the
  right (Grass/Poison), in Gen 1 and Gen 2.
- Fixed A-button action order (Stats, Switch, Relearn, Dismiss/Follow, TM field
  moves, HM field moves, Cancel; Gen 2 adds Move and Item under Stats).
- Modern Bag updated to Modern UI Suite 0.1.43's makeover: colour pictures for
  every named Gen 1/2 item, type-coloured TM/HM discs and new tab icons. Gen 1
  keeps Highlander's pocket-switch sound and the classic backpack picture.

## Borders and screen
- Custom Borders: Gold, Silver and Crystal Classic borders (20 choices).
- SCREEN RATIO (OFF / 16:9) runs the whole game in a 16:9 area on other screens.
- Window focus on launch when started from a frontend, Playnite or a shortcut.

## Quality of Life
- Game Corner sub-menu: Slot Odds, Bigger Payouts, All Lucky Slot M. and
  2x Coins for Cash, in Gen 1 and Gen 2.
- Blue into Green, Decapitalize Words fixes (Pokémon, Pokédex, play-time colon)
  and a longer list of words kept in capitals (PKMN, TMs, HMs, QoL, DVs...).
- Trainer Card shows the whole trainer picture again.

See CHANGELOG.md for every change, and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md for credits.
